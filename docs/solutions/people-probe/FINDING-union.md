# The two providers fail in uncorrelated ways. Use both.

## The split that settles it

For every buyer Exa found at Harbor IT and Ntiva, is that person in the drained
BrightData roster at all?

    present in the BrightData roster      22 of 25    88%
    truly absent                           3 of 25    12%
    of the 22 present, had a readable title  7        32%

So BrightData is NOT missing these people. It holds them and cannot read their title.
The 18.8% "recall" measured earlier was a title-extraction failure wearing the costume
of a coverage failure.

    BrightData  knows WHO works there.       88% complete, title readable ~32%
    Exa         knows WHAT they do.          titles correct, only 12-14 people per company

## The union

                      Harbor IT (206 emp)    Ntiva (445 emp)
    Exa alone                  13                  12
    BrightData alone           11                  19
    UNION                      20                  28
    overlap                     4                   3

The overlap is 4 of 20 and 3 of 28. The two sources are almost disjoint. That is the
whole point: they do not fail on the same people.

BrightData alone would have missed the COO, the Chief Services Officer, the Chief
People Officer and the Chief Transformation Officer.
Exa alone would have missed the CFO, the VP of Projects and Integrations, the Chief
Strategy Officer, the CTO and three Market Presidents.
