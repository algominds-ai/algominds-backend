## Decisions

1. A seller domain is not an ICP. Product, audience and buyer instructions for this ICP override general website positioning. Website facts cannot invent targeting boundaries.
2. Search angles explore the eligible population. They may vary without rewriting the profile and do not belong in the canonical account contract.
3. The frozen saved Ondato arm-seed note is the comparison authority; later evaluation fixtures are not. It names Founder/CEO without a fallback condition, requires at least one signal, and does not categorically exclude every mixed-channel company.
4. Reuse six completed outputs. Baseline has a confirmed AND-to-OR transformation; the simpler existing-shape output preserves the operator but adds proof/exclusion drift. Four prose sections preserve more meaning but still require another interpretation to enforce it.
5. Candidate to test: seller facts plus one scoped ICP, with required/preferred groups, alternatives within a group, and per-alternative source/time rules. No recursive expression language, persona taxonomy, stored query-angle list, or extra judge.
6. User correction: do not persist a campaign object or preselect campaigns at onboarding. Campaigns and angles are volatile derivatives of the ICP. Multiple ICPs under one account are useful only when product/audience/buyer intent differs; alternate queries do not create more ICPs.
