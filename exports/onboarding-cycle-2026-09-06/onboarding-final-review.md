# Onboarding final-output fidelity review

Scope: read-only review of all eight completed `final-controls` outputs in `exports/onboarding-cycle-2026-09-06/`, against `eval/arm-seed/ondato.note.txt` as the frozen comparison authority and `exports/onboarding-probe-2026-09-06/original-baseline.json`. No provider calls or production changes were made. The five `real-accounts-inputs` cases have inputs and page receipts but no corresponding final profile JSON, so they are not scored here.

## Original Ondato: improved structure, two material fidelity losses

The final profile correctly preserves the exact growth buyer roster and exclusions (`original-final.json:30-31`), hard company rules and revenue/funding OR (`:39-164`), and all five situations as one required `anyOf` group (`:167-235`). Each signal keeps its individual 12-month, 6-month, or 120-day value, and the launch/market/funding signals are marked as past events (`:172-230`). This fixes the baseline’s single soft trigger with `windowDays:365` (`exports/onboarding-probe-2026-09-06/original-baseline.json:67-71`) and its broad seniority-only buyer bands (`:28-35`).

1. **Leaky-funnel timing is misclassified.** The note says complaints are in reviews “from the last 6 months” (`eval/arm-seed/ondato.note.txt:7`), which makes the review publication/date of the review the relevant clock. The final record stores `appliesTo: "observation"` (`original-final.json:186-193`). A page revisited today could therefore make an old review look current. The baseline did not preserve any defensible basis because it collapsed all signals to 365 days (`original-baseline.json:67-71`).

2. **The global page-proof rule is not carried into three structured branches.** The frozen note requires every selected situation to be “proven by a page” (`eval/arm-seed/ondato.note.txt:5`). The market/license, consumer-launch, and funding conditions have `sourceRule: null` (`original-final.json:172-180`, `:200-208`, `:223-230`). Only the review source and age-law source retain explicit rules (`:186-216`). Their prose still mentions the events, but the structured output cannot enforce page evidence for those three branches. The original baseline at least labeled the combined trigger `proof: "page"`, but lost individual windows and event semantics (`original-baseline.json:67-71`); the final output trades that global proof field for better date structure and drops the proof requirement on those branches.

3. **Outbound-motion routing is stated too absolutely.** The note says private/reachable companies are for this outbound motion and that public companies/larger groups are named accounts outside it (`eval/arm-seed/ondato.note.txt:3`). The final turns that into a required “privately held company” condition (`original-final.json:121-135`) rather than preserving the named-account routing distinction. Treat this as a campaign-eligibility wording issue, not evidence that public companies are intrinsically invalid ICPs.

4. **One seller-origin phrase is an inference.** The final calls Ondato “Lithuania-founded” (`original-final.json:7`). The captured About page supports a London HQ, Lithuania R&D, founders’ fintech background, and a 2018 “Foundation of Ondato,” but does not literally state “Lithuania-founded” (`exports/onboarding-cycle-2026-09-06/final-controls-inputs.json:13`). This should be marked as derived or softened. The four named customers are not an unsupported-customer finding: each is paired with a corresponding case-study URL in the output (`original-final.json:8-26`).

## Other final controls

- **Compliance control passes the intended counterexample.** It keeps compliance-only buyer scope (`compliance-control-final.json:41-43`), the mandatory funding **AND** revenue pair (`:74-89`), no upper funding bound, and an optional launch (`:107-119`).
- **Cloudflare Access passes.** It keeps US/Canada OR, the four hard gates, and three optional signals with no invented window (`cloudflare-access-final.json:17-128`); the CISO ambiguity is explicitly left unknown (`:130-133`).
- **Snyk domain-only passes the unknown test.** With no note it leaves offer, buyer, and requirements empty and records the missing targeting choices (`snyk-domain-only-final.json:15-21`).
- **Deel preserves the mixed trigger Boolean and dates.** The optional expression remains `(90d hire AND 180d expansion) OR 60d replacement`, with event windows (`deel-composite-final.json:80-120`), and buyer remains null because no buyer role was supplied (`:19-21`, `:123-125`). The word “public” is retained in the replacement text but not a separate `sourceRule` (`:109-117`), so machine-level source enforcement is incomplete there too.
- **Toast preserves broad buyer functions and optional public signals** (`toast-operations-final.json:13-16`, `:73-95`). **Future-event** correctly distinguishes a 30-day publication clock from a 90-day future event (`future-event-final.json:48-70`).
- **Ondato optional control preserves optionality, open headcount, and the no-fallback buyer roster** (`ondato-optional-final.json:35-71`), but it assigns `appliesTo: "event"` to all three undifferentiated “within” signals (`:73-117`). That temporal basis is not specified by its note and should remain explicit unknown/text rather than an inferred event clock.
