This profile is for Trust Fabric only, not Form3's payments platform. Form3 (form3.tech) sells Trust Fabric, a product installed into a Kubernetes cluster to manage certificate trust and infrastructure risk across distributed services.
Hard gates, all required: runs production Kubernetes with a distributed architecture or many independent services (no cluster, no deal); an in-house engineering team operates that infrastructure, not an outsourced platform; 501 or more employees; uptime-critical or regulated, where an hour of downtime is a board-level event; based in North America, the UK and Ireland, or Continental Europe. Do not gate on revenue.
Verticals: SaaS, online retail, fintech, automotive, energy, pharma, gaming, media, travel, telcos, public sector. Model accounts at reachable size: Trainline, Skyscanner, Auto Trader UK, Ocado Technology, Deliveroo.
Out: consultants, agencies, non-profits, private equity, recruiters, freelancers, managed service providers and systems integrators, and certificate-lifecycle vendors such as AppViewX, Entrust and CyberArk.
The buyer is the platform, infrastructure or security leader who owns the cluster and its certificate estate, never the payments or commercial function: CTO, CIO, CISO, and the VP, Director or Head of Engineering, Platform Engineering, Infrastructure, Cloud, Site Reliability Engineering, DevOps, Security Engineering, Information Security or Architecture. CEO only at companies under about 1,000 people. Exclude recruiters, HR, sales, marketing, individual engineers and academics. At most three people per company.
Hot signals: a platform, SRE, infrastructure or PKI role posted in the last 30 days naming Kubernetes, TLS, mTLS, cert-manager, PKI or a service mesh; public evidence of production Kubernetes at scale; a published SLA; DORA, PSD2, NIS2 or PCI DSS obligations; a new platform or security leader in the last 12 months; a post-quantum commitment; a public certificate or TLS incident in the last 90 days.
