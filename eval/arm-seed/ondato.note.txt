Ondato sells identity verification (KYC, age assurance) as a conversion product: 97% average pass rate, 30-second onboarding, 192 countries, 10,000+ document types. We sell to growth buyers, not compliance. The buying committee is the CRO, Chief Growth Officer, CMO, Chief Product Officer, Head of Growth, Head of Product, Head of Business Development, and the Founder or CEO. Never target compliance, risk, legal or MLRO titles. Never pitch KYT (transaction monitoring).

Geography: United Kingdom and Ireland, United States, Canada. Verticals: fintech, neobanks, digital banks, payments companies and PSPs, lending, trading and investment apps, crypto exchanges and wallets, age-gated platforms (adult content, gambling, gaming, social, dating, vaping and alcohol e-commerce). A company qualifies by revenue or funding, never by headcount. Exclude verification vendors and competitors, compliance consultancies, agencies and non-profits. They are privately held and reachable for outbound: $2M to $250M raised or $1M to $150M in revenue; public companies and larger groups are named accounts outside this motion.

We target situations, not demographics. A company is a fit when at least one of these dated, publicly observable situations is true, and the situation must be proven by a page:
1. New market, new rulebook: announced a new market entry or a fresh e-money, banking, lending or payments license in the last 12 months, with a go-live in a new jurisdiction (UK, EU, US, Canada).
2. The leaky funnel: their own users publicly complain, in app store or Trustpilot reviews from the last 6 months, about failed, stuck or slow identity verification at signup.
3. Launch window: announced or launched a consumer product in the last 12 months that needs new users to verify identity or age at signup (a new app, wallet, card, account type or trading feature).
4. The age-check clock: an age-gated platform serving UK or US users, exposed to the UK Online Safety Act's age assurance duty or US state age-verification laws, shown by its own compliance statement or enforcement coverage.
5. Freshly funded: raised a round in the last 120 days with stated user-growth or expansion plans.

Hard rules for every company we find: it runs high-volume digital signups where identity assurance is a revenue enabler; it serves consumers or SMBs in the UK, Ireland, US or Canada; it is not itself a KYC, identity or age-verification vendor.
